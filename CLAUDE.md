# Development Rules & Guidelines

- No claim without proof: show real command output (analyze, tests, build, Supabase rows).
- Never print or commit secrets (.env, keys). Keep .env out of pubspec assets and out of git.
- No new features, no big rewrites, no deleting functionality to make a build pass.
- Do not hand-edit generated files; regenerate them.
- Ask me before any destructive change.
