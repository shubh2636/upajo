# Privacy Policy for Upajo (उपजो)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.upajo.app`  

---

## 1. Introduction
Upajo ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to empowering Indian farmers and consumers through a direct agricultural marketplace and AI farming tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when using our Android mobile application and web services.

We comply strictly with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** of India and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
1. **Account & Profile Data**: When registering via mobile OTP, we collect your phone number, full name, state, district, village or city, and pin code.
2. **Farmer Financial Information**: For farmers receiving direct payouts, bank account number, IFSC code, account holder name, and UPI VPA are collected and stored securely.
3. **Produce Listings & Images**: Produce photographs, crop varieties, harvest dates, and pack weights (10/20/50 kg).
4. **Plant Pathology Inquiries**: Leaf and crop photographs submitted to our AI Disease Scanner.
5. **Voice Audio Inputs**: Voice queries spoken into the "Krishi Sahayak" microphone are transcribed into text for agricultural advice.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
1. **Device & Telemetry Data**: Device model, Android OS version, approximate battery/network status, and crash logs (with personal data redacted).
2. **Location Data**: Precise or approximate GPS coordinates collected with your explicit runtime permission to compute distances to nearby APMC mandis and coordinate farm deliveries.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To facilitate direct transactions between farmers and consumers without intermediary fees.
- To provide scientific agronomic crop advice, weather warnings, and crop calendar tasks.
- To detect plant diseases and suggest organic and CIB&RC approved remedies.
- To route delivery drivers and calculate estimated delivery times.
- To generate official GST-exempt agricultural invoices under Section 23 of the CGST Act.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **No Data Selling**: We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to advertisers, marketers, or data brokers.
- **Transacting Parties**: When an order is placed, your delivery address and contact phone number are shared with the transacting farmer and delivery driver solely to fulfill the order.
- **Service Providers**:
  - **Razorpay**: For PCI-DSS compliant secure digital payments.
  - **Supabase**: Cloud database and authentication infrastructure.
  - **Open-Meteo**: For weather forecasts (transmits coordinates only; no user identifiers).

---

## 5. Security & Data Retention
- All data in transit is encrypted using **HTTPS / TLS 1.3**.
- Database records and cloud storage buckets are encrypted at rest with **AES-256**.
- Payment credentials are never stored on our servers.

---

## 6. User Rights & Data Deletion
In accordance with the DPDP Act, you have the right to review, update, or permanently delete your account and associated records:
- **In-App**: Navigate to `Profile -> Settings -> Delete My Account`.
- **Online Portal**: Visit `https://upajo.in/delete-account` or email `privacy@upajo.in`.
All personal data will be permanently wiped from active databases within 30 days of request receipt.
